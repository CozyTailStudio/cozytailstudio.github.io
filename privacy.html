3. Our games

Fern & Grid: Cozy Nonogram (early test version)

Fern & Grid: Cozy Nonogram (the "Game") is published by us. This section describes the early test version that we share with testers through Google Play. It is for people aged 16 and over.

  • No account. The Game has no account. We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts, photos or birth date.
  • On your phone. Your puzzle progress, collection, settings and choices are stored on your phone. We do not receive them. If the Android backup feature is on, the save file may be copied to your own Google account; that copy is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we receive only the confirmation that a purchase was made, so we can unlock it and restore it. Google Play Billing may send technical and purchase information to Google, which Google handles under its own privacy policy.
  • No advertising, no analytics. This version contains no advertising and no analytics software. It contains Google Play Billing software for purchases.
  • Google Play. If you install the Game from Google Play, Google processes your data under its own terms and privacy policy. Google also gives us technical reports, for example crash and not-responding reports from devices whose owners agreed to share diagnostics with Google, and install statistics. We use them only to fix problems.
  • Testers. If you take part in a test, we use the e-mail address you gave us only to add you to the tester list and to answer you. We remove the address from the list when the test ends and keep any e-mails you wrote to us for up to 12 months after the last message.
  • Children. The Game is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, write to info@cozytailstudio.com and we will delete it.
  • Deletion and questions. Write to info@cozytailstudio.com with the subject "Privacy request". Uninstalling the Game removes the data stored on your phone.

Before we publish a version that shows ads or collects any other data, we will update this section first and tell you in the Game where the law requires it.
